# Welcome to the Reg concert repository!

# Why does this exist?

Originally, setlists of Reg shows were preserved on setlist dot fm for people to see. Setlist dot fm is a wiki-style site, meaning anyone can go and edit the setlists, if they have knowledge of what happened. This includes completely deleting the shows...\
Back in June, all the shows done by Reg were deleted.\
To prevent something like that from happening again, the data had to be put onto something more secure. I also wanted to ensure other shows done on the server were archived. Hence, this site was born.

# Format of the site

There are two categories in this area:

- reg-sets, covering any show on the Elton John Salvation server done by Reg
- non-reg-sets, covering any show on the Elton John Salvation server done by a person other than Reg

Shows are listed chronologically.\
There is also a page dedicated to listing all the songs Reg has done in a live set, along with the number of times that the song was performed.

## Format of pages

Each show has a page dedicated to it. They will have the whole setlist, as well as any notes that the person that performed wishes to provide. An embed to the video on YouTube will also be provided (if available).

## Why can't I see the shows?

Recently, Reg's YouTube channel has been hit with two copyright strikes - one for Elton John's performance at Rock in Rio (Globo struck it), and one for Elton John's performance at the Apple Music Hall in London (Apple Music struck it).\
YouTube has a three strike rule. Three strikes = automatic channel termination.\
To prevent further issues, Reg has hid their channel, and no more videos will go out until at least December 23rd.\
This means that all the shows that were on Reg's channel are hidden and are inaccessible. The only shows that this doesn't impact are Joddman's September 4th show (the recording of this show is on his YouTube channel), Big Man's September 9th show (this was put up on Reg's channel, but was taken down pretty quickly before the copyright strikes because of an issue with one of the songs in the set, and it won't go up again (with the offending song removed) until he officially releases the original songs that feature in his set), and Dylan's August 16th show (which never went up on the channel).
