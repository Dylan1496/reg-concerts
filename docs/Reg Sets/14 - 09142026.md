**Notes**

- This is Reg's final show (for now). There was a show planned for the 16th but this was cancelled.
- Sacrifice is done in the same key as Elton did for RIR '26.
- Your Song was dedicated to all the people that ever attended a Reg/Bruh show.
- Don't Let The Sun Go Down On Me was dedicated to all the people we lost in 2025 and 2026 (including, but not limited to, Dolly Parton, Tim Curry, and the King of Norway).
- Bennie and the Jets was dedicated to Dylan.
- Why is Reg retiring? 2025 shows sucked because he didn't have time to rehearse. 2026 was similar at the beginning, but then he found time to rehearse. But good things must come to an end, and he wants to make sure things don't get out of hand.
- After the August 21st show Reg needed to say bye. Hence the two shows originally scheduled.
- Reg has to get better at singing and piano as well.
