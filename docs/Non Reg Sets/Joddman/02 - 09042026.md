**Notes**
- Celebrates the 20th anniversary of The Captain and the Kid's release. The setlist features all tracks from the album.
- All The Young Girls Love Alice was restarted due to the music cutting out.
- After Alice there was a break to resolve sound issues in the Discord.
- There was a short tease of Birds after Just Like Noah's Ark.
- There was a tease of both Mona Lisas and Mad Hatters (Parts 1 and 2) and Cold after Wouldn't Have You Any Other Way.
- Borat introduces The Bridge and I Must've Lost It on The Wind.
- Prior to Your Sister Can't Twist, Jay requested audience requests. There were none submitted while the song was playing, but Cold was requested by Surge after the song ended.
