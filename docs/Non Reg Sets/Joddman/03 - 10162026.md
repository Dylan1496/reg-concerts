**Notes**

- Celebrates the 25th anniversary of Songs From the West Coast. The setlist features all the songs from the album, done in order.
- Bennie has the falsettos in the third pre-chorus - this was a request of Gaming Legend.
- Birds was dedicated to Dylan. Jay stated that he rehearsed this more than any other song in the set.
- The Wasteland was going to be dedicated to Reg, but as he wasn't able to come to the show, Jay dedicated it to himself (?).
- Burn Down the Mission was dedicated to Ken (Dill Dandon), as it is his birthday.
- Crazy Water was also going to be dedicated to Reg. Again, couldn't come :(
- There was an interruption of about 10 seconds in Crazy Water, before it resumed.
